8 Secret Hidden Places in the Garden Route Most Travelers Miss

Beyond the standard N2 tourist trail lie secluded stone castles on the sand, gold rush ghost towns inside ancient forests, and prehistoric tidal caves.

Insider Secrets • 8 Hidden Spots • 8 Min Read • Moeketsi Motloung

1. Noetzie Beach & Stone Castles (Knysna)

Secret Beach • Castles

The Secret: Tucked at the end of a winding gravel road east of Knysna lies Noetzie, a quiet sheltered cove guarded by dramatic coastal cliffs. In the 1930s, eccentric families built actual stone castles directly overlooking the sand. Access is via a wooden stairway through indigenous milkwood forest—making it feel like stepping into a fairy tale.

💡 How to Reach: Take the Noetzie turnoff from the N2 just east of Knysna, park at the public lot, and descend the forested steps.

2. Jubilee Creek & Millwood Goldfields (Knysna Forest)

Forest Pool • 1880s Gold Rush

The Secret: In the 1880s, prospectors flocked to the Knysna forest during South Africa's first gold rush. Today, nature has reclaimed the ghost town of Millwood. Follow the 3.5 km Jubilee Creek trail through giant yellowwood trees to a pristine natural swimming pool and waterfall with amber mountain water.

💡 Local Tip: Pack a braai/picnic basket! SANParks provides peaceful stone picnic tables along the bubbling stream.

3. Gericke’s Point & Lion’s Head Tidal Pool (Sedgefield)

Fossil Dunes • Snorkeling

The Secret: Walk 2 km west along Swartvlei Beach at low tide to reach Gericke’s Point, an impressive fossilized sand dune shaped like a resting lion. Behind the sandstone peninsula lies a natural tidal rock pool so deep and sheltered you can snorkel among octopus, sea urchins, and colorful reef fish.

⚠️ Safety Tip: Visit only at low tide! High tide submerges the walking path along the cliffs.

4. Woodville 800-Year-Old Big Tree (Hoekwil)

Ancient Tree • Canopy Boardwalk

The Secret: While tourists crowd the Tsitsikamma Big Tree on the N2, the Woodville Big Tree near Hoekwil is a peaceful, rarely visited sanctuary. This magnificent Outeniqua Yellowwood is over 31 meters tall and has stood for over 800 years. A circular 2 km forest walk crosses crystal streams on elevated wooden boardwalks.

📍 Location: Hoekwil backroad, 25 mins from George

5. Pinnacle Point Point of Human Origins Caves (Mossel Bay)

Archaeology • Human Cradle

The Secret: Over 160,000 years ago during an Ice Age that threatened humanity with extinction, a small population of early modern humans survived in sea caves along the Mossel Bay cliffs. Guided by world-class archaeologists, visitors descend a series of cliffside wooden stairways directly into Cave 13B to see ancient ochre drawings and stone tool sites.

6. Prince Alfred’s Pass & Spitzkop Summit

Historic Pass • 360° Panorama

The Secret: At 68 km, this is the longest public mountain pass in South Africa, masterfully engineered by Thomas Bain in the 1860s. Halfway up, a steep forestry spur leads to Spitzkop (1,070m), the highest viewpoint along the entire Southern Cape coast where you can see from Knysna lagoon to the Swartberg mountains.

7. Goukamma Nature Reserve & Groenvlei Lake (Buffalo Bay)

Freshwater Lake • Wild Coast

The Secret: Groenvlei is the only natural freshwater lake in the Garden Route without an ocean inlet. Canoe peacefully through reed-lined waterways, hike over some of the highest vegetated coastal sand dunes in South Africa, or wander 14 km of empty marine sands where African Black Oystercatchers nest.
