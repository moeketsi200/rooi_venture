10 Must-Visit Attractions in George

George may be laid-back, but it's bursting with charm. Nestled in the heart of the Garden Route, this town is surrounded by mountains, ocean, and forest—making it a perfect hub for adventure, history, and family fun. Here’s a curated list of attractions that bring George to life:

1. Outeniqua Transport Museum

This vast hall houses a collection of historic steam locomotives, vintage cars, and railway coaches. Perfect for history buffs and children alike.

2. Victoria Bay

Flanked by cliffs and holiday homes, Victoria Bay is a cozy surf and swim spot perfect for a peaceful day by the ocean.

3. Redberry Farm

Pick strawberries, get lost in a giant hedge maze, enjoy pony rides, and eat sweet treats—fun for all ages.

4. Map of Africa

A natural formation in Wilderness resembling Africa—best seen from above with gorgeous valley and river views.

5. Garden Route Botanical Garden

Explore indigenous fynbos, scenic trails, a quiet dam, and an educational center nestled near the Outeniqua Mountains.

6. The Seven Passes Road

A historical scenic route between George and Knysna featuring winding mountain roads, bridges, and lush forest views.

7. Herold’s Wine Estate

Enjoy boutique wine tastings, vineyard walks, and peaceful mountain views in one of the smallest estates in the region.

8. Kaaimans River Bridge

A curved railway bridge across a scenic river mouth—great for photos and history lovers.

9. George Peak

A challenging hike up one of the region’s highest points with panoramic views over George and the coast.

10. Timberlake Organic Village

A family-friendly stop on the N2 with organic shops, kids’ play zones, and delicious food stalls.

Explore these gems and make the most of your stay in George — it’s more than just a stop along the Garden Route.
