15 Must-Visit Attractions in George: A Local's Guide

George is bursting with natural drama, historical charm, and outdoor wonder. Surrounded by the Outeniqua mountains, ancient forests, and the Indian Ocean—here are 15 essential attractions you cannot miss.

Garden Route Travel Guide • 15 Highlights • 9 Min Read • Moeketsi Motloung

1. Outeniqua Transport Museum

This vast hall houses an extraordinary collection of vintage steam locomotives, old passenger coaches, and antique cars. A must-see for history buffs and families.

2. Redberry Farm

Home to the largest hedge maze in the Southern Hemisphere! Pick sweet strawberries straight from the field, enjoy artisanal ice cream, and ride the miniature steam train.

3. Victoria Bay Cove

A snug, cliff-sheltered beach renowned for right-hand point break surfing and tidal rock pools. Grab a coffee at the beachfront cafe and watch dolphins play in the surf.

4. Herold’s Bay Tidal Pools

A quiet coastal hideaway just 15 minutes from George Airport. Known for safe sea swimming, sunbathing, and cliffside walking paths with seasonal whale viewing.

5. Montagu Pass Historic Road

Take a slow drive or cycle along this 1840s stone pass. The gravel road winds through deep fynbos ravines and past the old stone toll house.

6. Map of Africa Viewpoint

Marvel at the natural wonder where the Kaaimans River outlines Africa's shape, and watch paragliders launch effortlessly toward Wilderness beach.

7. Herold’s Wine Estate

A boutique high-altitude vineyard tucked on the northern slopes of the Outeniqua mountains. Enjoy cool-climate Pinot Noir and serene farm lunches.

8. Kaaimans River Gorge

One of the Garden Route's most iconic waterways. Rent a canoe to explore hidden waterfalls, or photograph the curved railway bridge overlooking the Indian Ocean.

9. George Peak Ascent

A classic mountaineering challenge starting near Witfontein. Reach the beacon for jaw-dropping panoramas above the clouds.

10. Timberlake Organic Village

An eco-friendly village situated between Wilderness and Sedgefield. Enjoy organic bakeries, local wine, craft coffee, and nature play gardens.

11. Garden Route Botanical Garden & Getafix Café

Nestled at the top of Caledon Street at the foot of the Outeniqua mountains, this 43-hectare botanical sanctuary is dedicated to preserving the rich floral biodiversity of the Cape Floral Kingdom. Stroll through the medicinal herb garden, relax by the open water dam with bird-watching hides, and savor coffee and freshly baked treats under the trees at the open-air Getafix Garden Café.

12. Witfontein Forest Reserve

The ultimate everyday outdoor escape for George locals. Located just minutes from town near the base of Montagu Pass, Witfontein offers pine-scented shaded trails, babbling mountain streams, and smooth singletracks beloved by trail runners, dog walkers, and mountain bikers. Keep an eye out for diverse forest fungi along the famous mushroom trail.

13. Touw River Lagoon & Wilderness Boardwalk

A short 10-minute coastal drive down Kaaimans Pass brings you to Wilderness, where the Touw River opens into a shimmering lagoon system. Hire a kayak or stand-up paddleboard to explore calm waterways, stroll the elevated wooden boardwalks surrounded by reed beds and waterbirds, and relax on the endless white-sand shoreline.

14. Historic Seven Passes Road (Bain's 1883 Route)

Before the modern N2 highway was carved, this 75-kilometer gravel pass was the only inland lifeline connecting George and Knysna. Engineered by Thomas Bain in 1883, it crosses seven river gorges over historic hand-crafted stone masonry bridges (including the Silver River and Kaaimans River bridges) through pristine indigenous forest canopies.

15. Groeneweide Forest Trails (Saasveld)

Situated around the historic Saasveld forestry campus on the eastern outskirts of George, the Groeneweide trail network winds deep into Afro-temperate canopy forests. Crossing clear amber mountain streams via wooden bridges, hikers can choose between 9 km, 11 km, and 13 km loop paths echoing with the resonant calls of the Knysna Turaco (Loerie).
