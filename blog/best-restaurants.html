Top 10 Scenic Restaurants & Foodie Spots in George & the Garden Route

From fresh ocean seafood platters perched over breaking surf to farm-to-table vineyard feasts in the mountains, here is our insider guide to eating well across the Garden Route.

Food & Dining Guide • George, Wilderness & Knysna • 7 Min Read • Moeketsi Motloung

1. The Fat Fish (George & Plettenberg Bay)

Seafood • Tapas

Why Go: Unquestionably one of the finest seafood restaurants in the Southern Cape. Enjoy pristine fresh linefish, hand-rolled sushi, plump Knysna oysters, and Spanish-style tapas in a chic, relaxed setting. Their wine list highlights the best boutique vineyards along the Cape south coast.

📍 Location: York Street, George Central & Central Beach, Plettenberg Bay

2. Salinas Beach Restaurant (Wilderness)

Beachfront • Sunset Views

Why Go: Perched directly over the sand dunes at the mouth of the Touw River, Salinas offers arguably the best ocean sunset view in Wilderness. Feast on peri-peri calamari, wood-roasted flatbreads, and grilled prawns while waves roll in just meters away.

📍 Location: Zandhoogte Road, Wilderness Beach (15 mins from George)

3. East Head Café (Knysna)

Lagoon Edge • Breakfast & Lunch

Why Go: Situated right at the foot of the Eastern Knysna Head, this cafe looks straight through the rocky channel where lagoon tides crash into the open ocean. Legendary for breakfast eggs benedict, beer-battered hake & chips, and iced artisanal lattes.

📍 Location: George Rex Drive, The Heads, Knysna

4. 101 Meade Street Bistro & Lounge (George Central)

Fine Bistro • Cocktails

Why Go: The culinary darling of downtown George. Helmed by passionate local chefs, 101 Meade pairs creative contemporary South African cooking with an intimate cocktail lounge. Think slow-braised Karoo springbok shank, truffle gnocchi, and decadent dark chocolate desserts.

📍 Location: 101 Meade St, George Central

5. Ristorante Enrico (Keurboomstrand, Plett)

Italian • Dolphin Spotting

Why Go: "Any closer and your feet would be wet!" Owner Enrico serves authentic Italian family recipes on a wide wooden deck looking directly into Keurbooms Bay. Watch schools of dolphins surf the waves while enjoying homemade seafood linguine and woodfired thin-crust pizza.

📍 Location: Main Beach, Keurboomstrand (near Plettenberg Bay)

6. Herold Wines Vineyard Lunches (Montagu Pass)

Vineyard • Mountain Air

Why Go: High on the northern slopes of the Outeniqua Mountains, this boutique vineyard serves hearty farm-style cheese and charcuterie platters, braai broodjies, and cellar-door tastings under walnut trees. A scenic reward after driving Montagu Pass!

📍 Location: Montagu Pass, Herold (30 mins from George)

7. The Girls on the Square (Wilderness)

Afro-Fusion • Tapas

Why Go: A beloved staple set in Wilderness village square. Celebrated for aromatic African-spiced coconut curry prawns, venison steaks, fresh oysters, and an open fairy-lit courtyard with live acoustic musicians.

📍 Location: Wilderness Square, Wilderness

8. Wild Oats Community Farmers' Market (Sedgefield)

Saturday Market • Artisan

Why Go: The most famous Saturday morning market in South Africa (7:30 AM – 12:00 PM). Wander beneath coastal trees with fresh filter coffee, German bratwurst, raw local honeys, goat cheeses, woodfired sourdough loaves, and savory breakfast pies.

📍 Location: N2, Sedgefield (25 mins from George)
