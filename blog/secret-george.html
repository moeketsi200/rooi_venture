10 Secret Spots in George Known Only to Locals

Escape the tourist crowds with off-the-beaten-path waterfalls, amber mountain pools, dramatic clifftop lookouts, and gravel passes that make George a hidden adventure paradise.

Garden Route Insider Guide • 10 Secret Spots • 8 Min Read • Moeketsi Motloung

1. Kaaimans River Grotto & Secret Waterfall

Tucked deep inside the Kaaimans canyon lies a hidden waterfall accessible only by kayak or a rocky river scramble. Surrounded by towering vertical cliff walls and hanging ferns, it feels worlds away from the nearby N2 highway.

2. The 1840s Montagu Pass

While modern traffic speeds over the paved Outeniqua Pass, the narrow gravel Montagu Pass winds quietly below. Marvel at hand-carved stone retaining walls, crystal mountain runoff cascades, and peaceful picnic spots with nobody in sight.

3. Garden Route Botanical Garden Trails

Located at the top of Caledon Street against the backdrop of the Outeniqua mountains, this peaceful garden features tranquil forest paths, a medicinal herb haven, bird hides, and an indigenous tree nursery.

4. The Local "Pepsi Pools"

Locals love this hidden rock pool along the mountain foothills. Named "Pepsi Pools" because of the rich dark amber color given off by natural fynbos tannins, the mountain water is completely pristine, icy cold, and exceptionally refreshing on summer afternoons.

Local Insight: The trail to Pepsi Pools is not heavily signposted. Ask local trail runners or join our guided hike to find it safely!

5. The Seven Passes Backroad

Constructed by master engineer Thomas Bain in the 1880s, the Seven Passes Road connects George to Knysna through rural farmlands, historic stone bridges, and ancient indigenous Yellowwood forests.

6. Gwaiing River Mouth & Tidal Lagoon

Located south of George along the coastal farmlands, the Gwaiing River carves through a dramatic sandstone ravine before emptying into the Indian Ocean. A sheltered tidal lagoon forms behind the beach at low tide, making it the locals' favorite secret enclave for peaceful weekend braais, paddleboarding, and coastal shore fishing without tourist crowds.

7. Voëlklip Sea Cliff Lookout (Herold’s Bay Heights)

Perched high on the rugged sea cliffs west of Herold's Bay, Voëlklip delivers a jaw-dropping ocean panorama hundreds of feet above crashing waves. It is George's most breathtaking vantage point for watching schools of dolphins surf the swells, witnessing spectacular coastal sunsets, and stargazing under pristine southern skies.

8. Silver River Gorge & 1880s Stone Arch Bridge

Draped in tree ferns along the gravel Seven Passes Road between George and Wilderness, the Silver River flows through a deep, quiet gorge spanned by a historic hand-chiseled stone masonry bridge. Adventurous locals love to scramble upstream over amber river boulders to find hidden pools and small cascading waterfalls.

9. Witfontein Secret Waterfall & Mushroom Path

While most casual walkers stick to the main gravel fire roads at Witfontein, a faint singletrack darts off into the indigenous yellowwood canopy. Follow the sound of trickling water to find a secluded mountain waterfall cascading into a mossy stone grotto with a lone wooden bench known only to dedicated local trail runners.

10. Ballots Bay & Nature Reserve

Concealed behind the mountain ridges just east of Victoria Bay, Ballots Bay is a secluded coastal nature reserve reached via a steep, exhilarating pass. The protected valley features private tidal rock pools, deep sea caves, and fynbos slopes where zebra, bushbuck, and guinea fowl roam freely along the rocky shoreline.
