Exploring the Best Kept Secrets of George

Discover the hidden gems that make George a true adventure paradise.

Introduction

George, nestled along South Africa’s scenic Garden Route, is often seen as a gateway town—but for those who take the time to explore, it’s a destination full of surprises. Whether you're a nature lover, a thrill seeker, or someone who simply enjoys quiet beauty, George offers adventures that many tourists overlook.

1. Kaaimans River Adventures

Hidden under the old railway bridge between Wilderness and George lies Kaaimans River, a local secret where you can kayak, swim, or just float under lush greenery. This is a perfect spot for anyone seeking peace and connection with nature.

2. The Montagu Pass

Take a scenic drive or hike along the historic Montagu Pass. Built in the 1840s, this gravel route winds through the Outeniqua Mountains and offers breathtaking views, waterfalls, and quiet picnic spots away from tourist traffic.

3. Garden Route Botanical Garden

This under-the-radar botanical garden showcases indigenous plant life and peaceful walking trails. Perfect for families, artists, or anyone needing inspiration from nature.

4. Pepsi Pools

Locals know about this hidden waterfall and rock pool located just off the Outeniqua hiking trail. It's called "Pepsi Pools" because of the dark, fizzy-looking water—totally safe and refreshing for a dip!

5. The Seven Passes Drive

Embark on a road less traveled—literally. The Seven Passes Road between George and Knysna is a scenic and historic alternative to the N2, offering forests, mountain views, and even ghost stories!

🌟 Local Insight: Ask locals for directions to Pepsi Pools—it’s not well marked but worth the effort!
