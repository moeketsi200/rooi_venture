Ultimate 10-Day Garden Route Road Trip Itinerary

10 unforgettable days driving along South Africa’s premier coastal route—connecting rugged coastlines, mountain passes, ancient forests, and semi-desert Karoo landscapes.

Garden Route Travel Guide • 10-Day Driving Plan • 12 Min Read • Moeketsi Motloung
Day 1: Mossel Bay Day 2: George Hub Day 3: Oudtshoorn & Karoo Day 4: Wilderness & Seven Passes Day 5: Sedgefield & Swartvlei Day 6: Knysna Lagoon Day 7: Noetzie Castles Day 8: Plettenberg Bay Day 9: Nature’s Valley Day 10: Tsitsikamma Road Trip Tips

Day 1: Mossel Bay – Maritime Heritage & Ocean Thrills

  • Early morning stroll along Santos Beach.
  • Explore the Bartolomeu Dias Museum Complex and the 500-year-old Post Office Tree.
  • Cage diving or fresh seafood lunch overlooking the waves at The Point.
Travel Tip: Shark cage diving trips depart early morning and should be reserved at least 48 hours ahead.

Day 2: George – Mountain Peaks & Forest Reserves

  • Hike the shady canopy loops of Witfontein Nature Reserve.
  • Discover vintage steam locomotives at the Outeniqua Transport Museum.
  • Sunset fish and chips while watching local surfers at Victoria Bay.

Day 3: Oudtshoorn & Montagu Pass – The Klein Karoo Loop

  • Ascend the historic 1840s stone-walled Montagu Pass into the high-altitude vineyards of Herold.
  • Descend into the sunny semi-desert Klein Karoo to explore the 20-million-year-old Cango Caves.
  • Tour historic Victorian ostrich feather palaces and savor sunset Klein Karoo wine and Karoo lamb.
Route Tip: Drive the gravel Montagu Pass northbound, and return via the tarred, fast Outeniqua Pass (N12) for dramatic views of George.

Day 4: Seven Passes Road & Wilderness – Waterways & Paragliding

  • Drive the historic 1883 Seven Passes Road through deep indigenous forest ravines.
  • Rent a canoe on the Touw River to paddle into the gorge and hike to the secret waterfall.
  • Admire paragliders taking off from the Map of Africa viewpoint and enjoy beachfront seafood at Salinas.

Day 5: Sedgefield & Swartvlei – Slow Town & Fossil Dunes

  • Experience the slow pace of Sedgefield, Africa’s first certified Slow Town.
  • Walk the low-tide beach to the 25,000-year-old fossilized dunes and snorkel pools at Gericke's Point.
  • If visiting on a Saturday, browse artisan sourdough and farm produce at the Wild Oats Community Farmers' Market.

Day 6: Knysna Lagoon – Catamaran Cruises & Clifftop Views

  • Take the scenic drive up to the Eastern Knysna Head lookout overlooking the ocean channel.
  • Board a ferry across the lagoon to hike the clifftop paths of Featherbed Nature Reserve.
  • Sample fresh wild oysters and chilled Sauvignon Blanc at the Knysna Quays Waterfront.

Day 7: Noetzie Beach & Ancient Goldfields – The Secret Coast

  • Discover the fairytale stone castles nestled on the wild sands of secluded Noetzie Beach.
  • Hike the Jubilee Creek trail through 1880s gold mining ghost town ruins to a natural forest pool.
  • Enjoy sunset coffee and light fare perched directly above the heads channel at East Head Café.

Day 8: Plettenberg Bay & Robberg – Marine Sanctuaries

  • Hike the dramatic peninsula loop of Robberg Nature Reserve past the Cape fur seal colony.
  • Walk through the forest canopy at Birds of Eden and Monkeyland in The Crags.
  • Dine on oceanfront Italian seafood right on the rocks at Ristorante Enrico in Keurboomstrand.

Day 9: Nature’s Valley & Bloukrans – Coastal Wilderness & Thrills

  • Drive down the winding, subtropical Groot River Pass into pristine Nature’s Valley.
  • Stroll the empty white sands where the world-famous Otter Trail comes to a close.
  • Visit the 216-meter Bloukrans Bridge—home to the world's highest commercial bridge bungee jump.

Day 10: Tsitsikamma National Park – The Ocean Grand Finale

  • Walk the famous 77-meter suspension bridges suspended over the ocean surf at Storms River Mouth.
  • Kayak or blackwater tube through the steep, narrow ravines of Storms River gorge.
  • Celebrate the completion of the ultimate Garden Route expedition with an oceanfront toast.

Essential Garden Route Road Trip Tips

Vehicle Selection

A standard sedan handles 90% of the route (N2 highway is beautifully paved). For historic gravel passes like Montagu Pass and Seven Passes, a high-clearance SUV provides added comfort and peace of mind.

Tolls & Cash

The Tsitsikamma Toll Plaza on the N2 between Plettenberg Bay and Storms River accepts credit cards and cash. Keep small denominations handy for SANParks entrance fees.

Best Travel Season

October to April brings glorious summer beach weather and warm waters. July to November offers prime coastal whale watching along Herold’s Bay, Plett, and Knysna.

Wildlife Etiquette

Baboons are common along Kaaimans Pass and Tsitsikamma rest stops. Never feed baboons, and always keep vehicle windows and doors locked when stopping at roadside view sites.
