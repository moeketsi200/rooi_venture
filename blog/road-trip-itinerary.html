Ultimate Garden Route Road Trip Itinerary

7 unforgettable days along South Africa’s most scenic adventure route.

🚗 Day 1: Mossel Bay

  • Sunrise beach walk.
  • Bartolomeu Dias Museum Complex.
  • Shark cage diving or seafood at The Point.
Travel Tip: Book shark cage diving at least 48 hours in advance!

🌄 Day 2: George

  • Hidden spots: Kaaimans River, Pepsi Pools.
  • Outeniqua Transport Museum.
  • Picnic at Victoria Bay or dinner in town.

🌲 Day 3: Wilderness

  • Kayak the Touw River.
  • Hike the Half-Collared Kingfisher Trail.
  • Stay in a treetop lodge.

🌉 Day 4: Knysna

  • Knysna Heads viewpoint.
  • Boat cruise on the lagoon.
  • Fresh oysters at the Waterfront.

🦓 Day 5: Plettenberg Bay

  • Robberg Nature Reserve hike.
  • Birds of Eden and Monkeyland.
  • Beach time at Lookout Beach.

🌳 Day 6: Nature’s Valley

  • Forest escape in a hidden paradise.
  • Hike part of the Otter Trail.
  • Reconnect with nature.

🌊 Day 7: Tsitsikamma & Storms River

  • Suspension bridge in Tsitsikamma National Park.
  • Ziplining or tubing in Storms River Gorge.
  • Stargazing by the ocean.
