Ultimate Garden Route Road Trip Itinerary

7 unforgettable days driving along South Africa’s most scenic adventure route, from historic Mossel Bay through the heart of George to Storms River.

Garden Route Travel Guide • 7-Day Driving Plan • 9 Min Read • Moeketsi Motloung
Day 1: Mossel Bay Day 2: George Hub Day 3: Wilderness Day 4: Knysna Day 5: Plettenberg Bay Day 6: Nature’s Valley Day 7: Tsitsikamma

Day 1: Mossel Bay – Maritime Heritage & Ocean Thrills

  • Early morning stroll along Santos Beach.
  • Explore the Bartolomeu Dias Museum Complex and the 500-year-old Post Office Tree.
  • Cage diving or fresh seafood lunch overlooking the waves at The Point.
Travel Tip: Shark cage diving trips depart early morning and should be reserved at least 48 hours ahead.

Day 2: George – Mountain Peaks & Forest Reserves

  • Hike the shady canopy loops of Witfontein Nature Reserve.
  • Discover vintage steam locomotives at the Outeniqua Transport Museum.
  • Sunset fish and chips while watching local surfers at Victoria Bay.

Day 3: Wilderness – Waterways & Paragliding

  • Rent a canoe on the Touw River and hike to the secret forest waterfall.
  • Stop by the Map of Africa viewpoint and watch tandem paragliders.
  • Dine at the bustling village sidewalk cafes in Wilderness town.

Day 4: Knysna – Estuary Cruises & Clifftop Views

  • Take the scenic drive up to the Eastern Knysna Head viewpoint.
  • Board a lagoon catamaran cruise out to the ocean channels.
  • Sample world-renowned Knysna wild oysters at the Quays Waterfront.

Day 5: Plettenberg Bay – Marine Sanctuaries & Peninsula Trails

  • Hike the dramatic peninsula loop of Robberg Nature Reserve past the Cape fur seal colony.
  • Walk through the forest canopy at Birds of Eden and Monkeyland.
  • Enjoy golden hour swimming at Lookout Beach.

Day 6: Nature’s Valley – Hidden Forest Sanctuary

  • Drive down the scenic winding Groot River Pass.
  • Relax on the completely undeveloped, pristine Nature’s Valley beach.
  • Walk the final stretch of the world-famous Otter Trail along the lagoon.

Day 7: Tsitsikamma & Storms River – The Grand Finale

  • Cross the thrilling 77-meter Storms River suspension bridge over the churning surf.
  • Blackwater tubing or canopy zipline through indigenous yellowwood forests.
  • Celebrate the journey with dinner overlooking the ocean breakers.
