16 Top Hiking Trails in George & Across the Garden Route

From George's towering mountain peaks and mossy afro-montane forests to the iconic coastal cliffs of Robberg, Tsitsikamma, and Knysna—here is your ultimate guide to hiking South Africa's Garden Route.

Garden Route Hiking Guide • 16 Trails • 11 Min Read • Moeketsi Motloung

1. Outeniqua Mountain Trail

Multi-Day • Moderate

What it is: This iconic trail stretches through the rugged Outeniqua Mountain Range with views of ancient valleys, indigenous forest pockets, and rich fynbos floral kingdoms. While popular as a multi-day trek, several sections can be enjoyed as exhilarating day hikes.

2. Witfontein Forest Trail

Day Loop • Easy

What it is: Situated just minutes from central George, Witfontein Nature Reserve features gentle shaded loop walks through towering pine plantations and lush native afro-montane forest. It is ideal for morning birdwatching, family strolls, and trail runners.

3. Cradock Peak

Summit • Challenging

What it is: The highest peak in the Outeniqua Range near George (1,578m). This strenuous day trek demands solid fitness and steady footing, but rewards you with breathtaking 360-degree vistas stretching from Mossel Bay to Knysna and deep into the semi-arid Karoo.

4. Groeneweide Trail

Forest Walk • Moderate

What it is: A shaded network of forest loops starting near Saasveld / Nelson Mandela University George Campus. Crystal-clear streams and dense canopy foliage make this one of the coolest hikes on warm summer days.

5. Herold’s Bay Cliff Path

Coastal • Easy/Moderate

What it is: A breathtaking coastal path hugging the rocky cliffs above Herold's Bay cove. During whale season (June to November), keep your eyes on the bay to spot Southern Right and Humpback whales breaching.

6. Map of Africa & Paraglide Launch

Viewpoint • Easy

What it is: Located atop the Wilderness Heights ridge, the Kaaimans River meanders naturally around a hill to outline the geographic shape of the African continent. Nearby trails wind down to the river mouth.

7. Montagu Pass Historic Hike

Heritage • Moderate

What it is: Walk along South Africa's oldest unaltered stone pass built by convicts in the 1840s. Dry-stone retaining walls, old toll houses, and dramatic gorge drops make this a hike rich in history.

8. Fern Forest Trail

Canopy Walk • Easy

What it is: A serene walk surrounded by giant tree ferns, moss-covered boulders, and trickling streams. Perfect for nature photographers and peaceful forest bathing.

9. Victoria Bay Coastal Walk

Ocean • Easy

What it is: A picturesque stroll along the ocean pathway of Victoria Bay cove. Watch surfers carve world-class point waves while exploring natural rock pools and wave-carved caves.

10. Kaaimans River Trail & Falls

River • Moderate

What it is: Combining riverside paths with kayak access, the Kaaimans trail leads into deep amber-colored canyon waters surrounded by towering vertical rock cliffs and hidden natural swim holes.

11. Robberg Nature Reserve Peninsula Loop (Plettenberg Bay)

Peninsula • Moderate/Challenging

What it is: Globally acclaimed as one of South Africa's most breathtaking coastal day treks, this 9 km loop navigates a rocky marine peninsula jutting out into the Indian Ocean. Hike past a barking colony of thousands of Cape fur seals, descend the shifting sand amphitheater of the great Witzand dune, and cross the tombolo sandspit to explore The Island.

12. Half-Collared Kingfisher Trail & Waterfall (Wilderness)

River Gorge • Moderate

What it is: Winding alongside the peaceful Touw River inside Wilderness National Park, this 7 km return walk features an entertaining hand-pulled wooden pontoon river crossing. Elevated wooden boardwalks guide you through dense indigenous yellowwood canopy and giant tree ferns to a tranquil mountain rock pool and waterfall.

13. Tsitsikamma Suspension Bridges & Waterfall Trail

Marine Coast • Moderate

What it is: Experience where the wild Tsitsikamma indigenous forest collides with the churning Indian Ocean. Walk across the thrilling 77-meter suspension bridge dangling over the stormy ocean mouth of Storms River, or hike the first 3 km of the world-renowned Otter Trail across rocky tidal shelves to a coastal cave and roaring waterfall emptying into the sea.

14. Jubilee Creek Trail & Forest Pool (Knysna Forest)

Forest Stream • Easy

What it is: A tranquil 3.6 km shaded trail tracing the amber-tinted waters of Jubilee Creek in the Gouna forest near Knysna. Walk under ancient Outeniqua yellowwoods past historic 1880s Millwood gold-mining relics before arriving at an idyllic natural swimming pool nestled beneath moss-draped boulders.

15. Nature’s Valley & Salt River Coastal Loop

Wild Coast • Moderate

What it is: One of the Garden Route’s finest untouched coastal walks. Starting at the mouth of the Groot River lagoon in Nature’s Valley, the trail climbs forested coastal bluffs with sweeping vistas over the Tsitsikamma marine reserve before dropping into the wild, secluded pebble-and-sand cove of the Salt River mouth.

16. Featherbed Trail (Knysna Heads)

Lagoon & Cliffs • Moderate

What it is: Reached via a ferry across the Knysna Lagoon, this premier South African heritage trail follows clifftop pathways across the Western Knysna Head. Wander through coastal fynbos and ancient sea-carved caves while gazing down at the dramatic channel where the lagoon meets the open ocean.
