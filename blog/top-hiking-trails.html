Top 10 Hiking Trails in and Around George

Home About Blog Contact

George is a paradise for outdoor lovers! Whether you're a casual stroller or a seasoned hiker, this region offers a range of breathtaking trails. Dive into our detailed guide to find your perfect hike:

1. Outeniqua Trail

What: This iconic multi-day hike stretches about 26 km through the rugged Outeniqua Mountains...

2. Witfontein Trail

What: A peaceful loop trail through fynbos and forest.

3. Cradock Peak

What: A tough climb to George’s highest point with epic views.

4. Groeneweide Trail

What: A shaded, flat trail ideal for birdwatching.

5. Herold’s Bay Cliff Path

What: Scenic cliffside walk with ocean views and whale sightings.

6. Map of Africa Viewpoint

What: Famous viewpoint shaped like Africa.

7. Montagu Pass Hike

What: A historic trail along a 19th-century pass.

8. Fern Trail

What: Green and shady forest path with ferns.

9. Victoria Bay Walk

What: Coastal walk with tide pools and surfer views.

10. Kaaimans River Trail

What: A peaceful trail by the river with falls and swim spots.
