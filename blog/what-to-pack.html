What to Pack for the Garden Route

A Smart Traveler’s Guide – Everything you need for a smooth, scenic journey through George, Knysna, Tsitsikamma, and beyond.

Updated 2025 • Gear & Preparation • 5 Min Read • Moeketsi Motloung

Essential Items for Every Traveler

Must-haves before you set foot on the trail or road:

  • Passport / South African ID: Required for airport security and nature reserve registration.
  • Travel Insurance Documents: Always recommended for outdoor hiking adventures.
  • Compact Daypack (15–25L): With comfortable chest and waist straps for day hikes.
  • Comfortable Walking Shoes: Sturdy trainers or lightweight trail running shoes.
  • Reusable Water Bottle: Stay hydrated and avoid single-use plastics along trails.
  • Portable Power Bank: Mountain GPS and photography drain battery quickly.
  • Card & Small Cash: Card is accepted widely, but small town farmstalls and tip spots appreciate cash.

Seasonal Tips: Summer vs. Winter

Summer (Nov – March)

Warm & sunny days with afternoon coastal breezes:

  • Breathable lightweight UV clothing
  • Wide-brim sunhat & polarized sunglasses
  • SPF 50+ mineral reef-safe sunscreen
  • Swimwear & quick-dry microfiber towel
  • Insect repellent for river walks

Winter (June – August)

Crisp mornings and misty mountain passes:

  • Thermal base layer for early summit climbs
  • Insulating fleece or lightweight puffer jacket
  • Waterproof & windproof rain shell
  • Warm beanie & lightweight gloves
  • Moisture-wicking merino wool hiking socks

Adventure & Hiking Gear

  • High-Traction Hiking Boots: Essential for rocky peaks like Cradock and Outeniqua.
  • Lightweight Rain Poncho or Shell: Coastal Garden Route weather can shift unexpectedly.
  • Headlamp or LED Torch: With spare batteries for early sunrise starts or late descents.
  • Compact First Aid Kit: Antiseptic wipes, blister plasters, pain relief, and rehydration salts.
  • High-Energy Trail Snacks: Biltong, nuts, dried mango, and electrolyte tablets.
  • Offline Navigation Maps: Pre-download AllTrails or Google Maps (mobile signal is patchy in forests).

Beach & Lagoon Must-Haves

  • Sand-free beach blanket or compact camp chairs.
  • Waterproof dry-bag (10L) for kayaking Kaaimans River or Wilderness Touw River.
  • Snorkel mask and water shoes for tidal pools at Herold’s Bay and Victoria Bay.
  • Insulated cooler pouch for cold drinks and lunch.

Sustainable & Leave-No-Trace Travel

The Garden Route is part of the fragile Cape Floral Kingdom. Please follow these simple guidelines:

  • Pack it in, pack it out: Always carry your trash back from trails and beaches.
  • Stick to marked trails: Protect vulnerable fynbos species from erosion.
  • Respect wildlife: Do not feed baboons or disturb nesting marine birds.

What to Leave at Home

  • Flashy jewelry or expensive watches that attract unnecessary attention or could be lost on trails.
  • Bulky hard-shell luggage if you are doing multi-destination road trips (duffel bags fit boot space much better).
  • Overly formal clothes — Garden Route restaurants and activities are relaxed, friendly, and casual.
