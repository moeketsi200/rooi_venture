🧳 Essential Items for Every Traveler

  • Passport or ID (for local flights)
  • Travel insurance documents
  • Daypack for excursions
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Chargers, power bank & travel adapter
  • Cash & card (some small towns are cash-preferred)

🌦 Seasonal Tips: What to Pack for Summer vs Winter

Summer (Nov–Mar):

Warm & sunny, with occasional rain near Tsitsikamma.

  • Lightweight clothing
  • Sunhat, sunglasses & SPF
  • Swimsuit & flip-flops
  • Bug spray (for coastal hikes)

Winter (Jun–Aug):

Cool mornings in George, misty Knysna forests, but still great for travel!

  • Layers (hoodie, fleece, waterproof jacket)
  • Beanie or scarf for early hikes
  • Comfortable boots

🥾 Adventure Gear: For Hikers and Outdoor Lovers

  • Hiking boots or trail shoes
  • Quick-dry clothing
  • Headlamp or flashlight
  • First-aid kit
  • Snacks or trail mix
  • Map or offline GPS app (signal can be patchy in forests)

🏖 Beach Day Must-Haves

  • Beach towel or picnic blanket
  • Swimwear & cover-up
  • Cooler bag with drinks
  • Beach umbrella or tent (optional)

🌱 Eco-Friendly Packing Tips

Help keep the Garden Route pristine by packing consciously:

  • Reusable utensils & containers
  • Canvas tote bags
  • Biodegradable toiletries
  • Refillable water bottle

📦 Bonus: What NOT to Pack

  • Expensive jewelry – it’s a casual vibe here
  • Heavy books – bring a Kindle or download offline maps
  • Too many formal clothes – it’s all about comfort
