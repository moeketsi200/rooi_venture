Exploring the Garden Route: Best Day Trips from George

Based in George? Lucky you. There are amazing day trips all within an hour or two. Here are my top picks:

  • Knysna – Lagoon cruises, the Knysna Heads, and oysters.
  • Wilderness – Canoeing, paragliding, and pristine beaches.
  • Oudtshoorn – Cango Caves and ostrich farms await.
  • Hartenbos – A family-friendly beach spot with a holiday vibe.
  • Tsitsikamma National Park – Dramatic coastlines and hikes.
  • Mossel Bay – Shark cage diving, beaches, and museums.
  • Great Brak River – A small town feel with big scenery.

These trips give you a taste of everything — nature, culture, and fun. George is the perfect launchpad!
