8 Best Day Trips from George: Exploring the Garden Route

Based in George? You are situated in the ultimate Southern Cape launchpad. Here are 8 incredible day excursions located within a scenic 15-minute to 2-hour drive—from coastal marine peninsulas to mountain cave wonderlands.

Garden Route Travel Guide • 8 Day Trips • 8 Min Read • Moeketsi Motloung

1. Wilderness & Touw River Lagoon (15 mins from George)

Wilderness is George’s neighboring eco-wonderland. Rent a canoe on the Touw River to paddle to the Half-Collared Kingfisher Trail waterfall, relax on golden endless beaches, or watch paragliders soar along the coastline.

2. Knysna Heads & Waterfront (55 mins from George)

Famous for the dramatic sandstone cliffs guarding its tranquil lagoon mouth. Enjoy fresh oysters at the vibrant waterfront, take a ferry across the lagoon, or walk along Coney Glen beach.

3. Tsitsikamma National Park & Storms River (1h 45m from George)

Where ancient forest meets the stormy Indian Ocean. Walk across the thrilling Storms River suspension bridge, kayak through deep gorge ravines, or hike the first section of the world-famous Otter Trail.

4. Mossel Bay & The Point (40 mins from George)

Visit the historic Post Office Tree at the Bartolomeu Dias Museum Complex, ride the world's longest over-ocean zipline, and explore tidal rock pools along The Point.

5. Oudtshoorn & Cango Caves (55 mins from George)

Drive north through the breathtaking Outeniqua Pass, leaving lush coastal forests behind to enter the sun-drenched semi-desert of the Klein Karoo. Delve deep underground into the 20-million-year-old limestone dripstone wonderland of the Cango Caves, admire historic 19th-century sandstone ostrich palaces, and sample regional wines and artisanal biltong.

6. Plettenberg Bay & Robberg Peninsula (1h 15m from George)

Widely celebrated as the coastal crown jewel of the Garden Route, Plettenberg Bay combines pristine blue-flag beaches with dramatic marine biodiversity. Hike the prehistoric rocky headlands of the Robberg Peninsula to watch thousands of Cape fur seals play in the surf, take an ocean charter to spot Bottlenose dolphins and whales, or visit Monkeyland in The Crags.

7. Sedgefield & Swartvlei Lagoon (35 mins from George)

Africa’s first certified "Slow Town", Sedgefield is a tranquil natural retreat positioned between rolling sand dunes and the expansive Swartvlei estuary. Paddle calm lagoon channels on a kayak, walk along the beach to the ancient fossilized dunes of Gericke's Point, and time your trip for Saturday to experience the legendary Wild Oats Community Farmers' Market.

8. Nature's Valley & Coastal Wilderness (1h 35m from George)

The only settlement entirely nestled within the borders of the Tsitsikamma section of the Garden Route National Park. Mark the finish line of the world-famous Otter Trail, relax on peaceful, uncrowded sands sheltered by towering indigenous headlands, and canoe up the mirror-like, amber waters of the Groot River estuary.
