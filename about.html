Founder & Mission

Inspiring Exploration Along the Garden Route

Connecting travelers, local communities, and the natural wonderland of George, South Africa.

Moeketsi Isaac Rooi Motloung

Tourism Student • Trail Guide • Garden Route Enthusiast

Hi! I'm Moeketsi Isaac Rooi Motloung, a passionate tourism student based in the picturesque coastal city of George, Western Cape. I founded Rooi Venture to share hidden gems, unforgettable hiking trails, and travel inspiration across the Garden Route and beyond. Whether you are a seasoned adventurer scaling mountain peaks or a first-time visitor craving peaceful coastal forests, Rooi Venture is your dedicated companion.

Our Mission

  • Craft unforgettable adventures that spark joy, build resilience, and foster connection with nature.
  • Empower individuals to step out of their comfort zones and discover new outdoor passions.
  • Curate immersive trail experiences that inspire personal growth and wellness.
  • Connect travelers with the natural world, promoting a sense of wonder and environmental care.

Our Purpose

  • Inspiring a lifelong love of adventure, discovery, and mindful exploration.
  • Building a welcoming community of outdoor enthusiasts and conscious travelers.
  • Fostering empathy and appreciation between diverse people, places, and local cultures.
  • Highlighting George as the ultimate eco-tourism and adventure capital of South Africa.

Core Values

  • Sustainable Tourism: Minimizing ecological footprint and preserving pristine fynbos ecosystems.
  • Community Support: Partnering with local businesses, guides, and artisans across the Garden Route.
  • Safety & Respect: Prioritizing trail safety, preparedness, and cultural respect on every excursion.
  • Inclusivity: Making outdoor recreation accessible and enjoyable for adventurers of all backgrounds.
