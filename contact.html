Trail FAQ

When is the best time to hike in George? Year-round! Spring (Sept-Nov) and Autumn (March-May) feature mild temperatures and blooming fynbos flowers.
Are hiking permits required? Some protected nature reserves (like CapeNature and SANParks) require day permits. We provide all guidance upon inquiry.
